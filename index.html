If your PC or laptop is turned off, in sleep mode, restarting, disconnected from the internet, or otherwise offline, the EA cannot execute new trades or manage existing positions during that period.

For more consistent operation, we recommend running MetaTrader 5 on a reliable Windows VPS with a stable internet connection.
