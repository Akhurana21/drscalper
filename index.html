How Dr. Scalper EA Works

One structured cycle. Automated execution.

Disciplined trade management for consistent results.

01

Precision Entry System

Dr. Scalper EA automatically identifies trading opportunities and executes trades according to its predefined strategy.

02

Unified Position Management

Dr. Scalper EA manages multiple positions collectively as a basket, applying structured trade management across the overall position.

03

Dynamic Recovery Management

The EA uses predefined recovery and position management rules to systematically manage trades during changing market conditions.

04

Intelligent Exit System

Predefined exit rules automatically manage take-profit levels and trade closures, maintaining a systematic approach to position management.

05

After completing a trading cycle, the EA automatically prepares for the next opportunity according to its predefined rules.

Dr. Scalper EA can temporarily pause new entries during predefined periods while continuing to manage existing positions.
